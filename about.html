Our Story

Faith, Family & Service Since 1982

Building a community rooted in faith, love, and service — one Sabbath at a time.

Who We Are

A Home for Every Generation

Abeka SDA Church was founded in 1982 with a vision to create a welcoming spiritual home. Today we serve a growing family of members through worship, community outreach, and discipleship programs.

Our Mission

  • Preach the Gospel — sharing the good news of Jesus Christ with our city and beyond.
  • Foster a Loving Community — a family where every member is known, cared for, and supported.
  • Nurture Spiritual Growth — through prayer, Bible study, and discipleship at every stage of life.
  • Serve Our Local Community — extending Christ's love through outreach and practical service.
What We Believe

Our Core Beliefs

Biblical Authority

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and our final authority for faith and life.

Salvation

We believe in salvation through faith in Jesus Christ alone, by God's grace.

Community

We believe the Church is God's family, gathered together for worship and spiritual growth.
