Our Clubs

Our Youth Ministries

Explore the various Adventist Youth Ministries we offer to engage and empower young people in their faith journey.

Ages 4–9

Adehyɛ Adventurer Club

Little Hands, Big Hearts, Growing with Jesus

“Adehyɛ” means “Royal” and reflects our belief that every child is a prince or princess in God's Kingdom. Our club is where their royal journey begins — a foundation of interactive learning, simple crafts, and singing that sets the stage for their future in the Royal Pathfinder Club.

Read More
Ages 10–15

Royal Pathfinder Club

Courage for the Adventurer, Faith for the Journey

As Royal Pathfinders, we carry the name with honor and duty — known for our discipline, uniforms, and camporees. Our club is a training ground where we build lifelong friendships and learn that true royalty in God's eyes means serving with courage and integrity.

Read More
Ages 16–21+

Royal Ambassador Club

Faith in Action. Leadership for Life.

Royal Ambassadors are the legacy leaders of our youth ministry. The focus shifts to mentorship and strategic service — directing and counseling the younger clubs, and engaging in peer-driven Bible study, life-skills workshops, and mission trips.

Read More
Ages 22–35+

Young Adults Ministry

Connect. Grow. Impact.

The Young Adults Ministry is the next step after the Ambassador Club — designed for young professionals and students to connect in fellowship, grow in discipleship, and make a lasting impact in the Church and the world.
