About the Club

Adehyɛ Adventurer Club

“Adehyɛ” means “Royal” and reflects our belief that every child is a prince or princess in God's Kingdom. Our club is where their royal journey begins! It's a foundation of interactive learning, simple crafts, and singing, setting the stage for their future in the Royal Pathfinder Club.

Vision

To see every child at SDA Church-Abeka feel the unconditional love of Jesus and develop a lifelong, joyful relationship with Him.

Mission

The Adehyɛ Adventurer Club strengthens parent-child relationships by providing specialized, age-appropriate programming that fosters a sense of wonder, belonging, and achievement. We introduce children to God's world, the joy of serving others, and the basics of Christian living through play, creativity, and discovery.

Core Values

  • Love for Jesus — Making faith fun, relatable, and foundational.
  • Discovery — Nurturing a sense of wonder for God's creation and His word.
  • Family — Partnering with parents as the primary spiritual guides.
  • Joy — Creating a happy, safe, and playful environment.
  • Belonging — Helping each child feel accepted and valued in the Abeka SDA family.
