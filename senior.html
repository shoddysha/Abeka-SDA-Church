About the Club

Ambassador

Royal Ambassadors are the legacy leaders of our youth ministry. The focus shifts to applying our skills in mentorship and strategic service. We serve as directors and counselors for the younger clubs and engage in peer-driven, deep Bible study, life-skills workshops, and ambitious mission trips. This club ensures that the “Royal” legacy continues by empowering us to be active, faith-filled leaders in the Church and the world.

Vision

To cultivate a generation of young adult leaders from Abeka who live out their faith with purpose, transforming their world through service, discipleship, and authentic Christian witness.

Mission

The Royal Ambassador Club is a post-Pathfinder ministry that challenges young adults to own their faith and transition from being participants to becoming mentors and leaders. We provide a platform for spiritual growth, deep relationships, and practical service projects that address real-world needs.

Core Values

  • Ownership — Taking personal responsibility for one's spiritual journey and impact.
  • Leadership — Developing skills to mentor the next generation of Royals.
  • Service — Engaging in meaningful, hands-on mission and community projects in and beyond Abeka SDA Church.
  • Authenticity — Fostering a genuine and transparent community of believers.
  • Legacy — Investing in the future of the Adehyɛ Adventurer and Royal Pathfinder Clubs.
